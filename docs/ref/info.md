Events

Due to the large number of athletics events that are regularly contested, results and statistics often use abbreviated event names. The following list includes established and historical abbreviations; the new senior race-walk distances adopted in 2026 are identified by their full names where a standard abbreviated form has not been verified. Event codes are grouped by discipline for ease of reference.
